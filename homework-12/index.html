Sound Player

With this work I was attempting to experiment with a bunch of features I had not touched in p5.js, this is largely focused on sound but I also added text for the first time and used button controls for the first time as well. With it find a sound project I wanted to take some inspiration from music genres I enjoy as well as from rhythm game selection screens.

Sound one is "Guitar Song Loop #1 Connect Us by KLEBER KGF", Sound two is "electric_bassline.wav", Sound three is "Smooth Electric Piano Loop 123BPM", and Sound four is "Live Drums, Punchy & Compressed (Ride) - 95bpm"

All sounds were collected from freesound.org
